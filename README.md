## Research Experience

### Oceanic AI Fellowship | Computer Vision Research

*Commonwealth Cyber Initiative · William & Mary · Virginia Institute of Marine Science*

Worked with a four-person research team to develop and evaluate computer vision methods for underwater HabCam imagery across **six survey years**. We trained and compared YOLOv8s object detection models, investigated changes in performance caused by differences in imaging conditions, and added **200 manually annotated images** to improve representation of more challenging survey data.

Our evaluation included precision, recall, and mean average precision, as well as preliminary segmentation-based size estimation with MobileSAM. A retrained model achieved **0.872 all-class mAP@0.5**, and our team received the **Best Project Award in the Computer Vision Track**.
